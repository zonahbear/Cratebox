Get Cratebox on your iPhone

Takes just a few minutes, and you only do it once. Use your Apple ID email (find it in Settings → your name).

  1. Ask for an inviteFill in the form on this page. Invites are approved by hand.
  2. Accept Apple's emailOnce approved, Apple emails you. Tap Accept Invitation and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Get TestFlightIt's Apple's free app for beta apps. Download it from the App Store.
  4. Open the TestFlight emailIt may take a moment longer than the first one. Open it on your iPhone and tap View in TestFlight.
  5. InstallTap Accept, then Install.
  6. Open CrateboxFrom TestFlight or your home screen. Start mixing.

No email? Check spam, or just open TestFlight. Cratebox may already be waiting there.
