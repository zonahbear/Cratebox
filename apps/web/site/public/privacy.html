Privacy

The web player

Cratebox plays music from folders you pick on your own computer or phone. Your music, playlists and analysis stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no ads or trackers.

The iOS beta signup

When you ask for an invite we keep your first name, last name and email, the date you asked, and where your request is up to. That is all.

  • It's stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site.
  • Once you're approved, it's sent to Apple App Store Connect to invite you to the Cratebox team and TestFlight. Apple's own privacy policy covers it from there.
  • It's never sold or shared with anyone else, and it isn't used for marketing.

Delete your data

Enter the email you signed up with. Your signup is deleted right away, and you're removed from the App Store Connect team and TestFlight within a few days.
